ZapSound — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

ZapSound ("the app") plays a sound when you plug in or unplug your device's charger, with optional battery-full alerts and an anti-theft alarm. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. ZapSound does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.

Data stored on your device only

  • Settings — your chosen sounds, volume, quiet hours, and other preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Voice recordings — if you record a custom sound, the recording is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove it or uninstall the app.
  • Anti-theft PIN — stored locally on your device only.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone — only when you choose to record a custom sound. Recording happens only while the recording screen is open.
  • Notifications — for the background-service notice and the anti-theft alarm screen.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — so charger sounds can play while the app is closed.

Purchases

The optional one-time "ZapSound Pro" upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment data.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
